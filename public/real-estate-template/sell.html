The process

Five steps from valuation to completion

Typical timeline: 19 days to an accepted offer, 41 days to completion. Preparation adds two to three weeks if you want it to.

1

Valuation & strategy (week 0)

On-site visit, written valuation, comparable sales pack and an honest conversation about timing.

2

Preparation (weeks 1–3)

Only the work that returns more than it costs: paint, lighting, hardware, decluttering, landscaping. We project-manage the trades.

3

Photography & launch (week 3)

Half a day of photography and film, floor plans drawn, dossier assembled, then a coordinated launch across every channel.

4

Viewings & offers (weeks 4–6)

Accompanied viewings, a set offer deadline where the interest justifies one, and every offer presented on a single comparison page.

5

Inspection to completion (weeks 6–11)

We manage the inspection, the lender, the title company and the movers. You get a written update every Friday.

01 — Buyer representation

Buyers only. It sounds obvious; almost nobody does it.

Dana Kim built our buyer practice on a single rule: we never list the property you are buying. No dual agency, no quiet pressure to accept a colleague's offer, no split incentive. Your agent is paid to get you the house at the lowest defensible price.

  • Pre-underwriting introductions so your offer reads as certain, not hopeful
  • Inspection reports read line by line with you, not skimmed
  • Off-market access — about a third of what we sell never reaches the portals
  • Escalation clauses, rent-backs and inspection strategy explained before you need them

What it costs: in most transactions the buyer's side of the commission is paid from the seller's proceeds. Where a fee applies we confirm it in writing before you view a property.

03 — Investment & 1031

We show you the spreadsheet before you ask for it

Samuel Lowe underwrites every investment listing himself: rent roll, expense history, capex schedule, vacancy assumptions and a downside case. If the numbers don't work, we tell you before you make an offer — including on our own listings.

  • 41 like-kind exchanges completed with two local intermediaries

05 — Probate & estates

Selling a home you'd rather not deal with

Inherited, distant, full of forty years of belongings, or mid-repair — we take these on regularly and we do not flinch at them.

  • Clearing and donation coordination, including furniture and tools
  • Trusted trades at trade prices for essential repairs only
  • Access management — you do not need to be in the state
  • Liaison with attorneys, executors and out-of-state beneficiaries
  • Written cost schedule before anything is committed
  • Discreet marketing where a family requires it

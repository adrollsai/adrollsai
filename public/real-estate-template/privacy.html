Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025. This is template copy — have it reviewed by a qualified attorney before you publish.

1. Who we are

Everline Realty ("we", "us") is a licensed real estate brokerage operating from 2140 Alder Street, Suite 300, Riverton, CA 94107. You can reach us at hello@everlinerealty.com or on (555) 018-2245.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and to carry out a transaction you have asked us to handle:

  • Contact details you submit through our forms — name, email address, phone number.
  • Property details you provide when requesting a valuation, such as an address and any notes about condition.
  • Search criteria you give us, so that we can send you matching listings.
  • Technical information your browser sends automatically, such as page views and referral source, in aggregate.
  • Documents required to complete a transaction, where you have engaged us to act for you.

3. Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, to provide the service you requested, to keep records required of a licensed brokerage, and — only where you have opted in — to send you our monthly market note.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with parties required to complete a transaction you have asked for: the other side's agent, escrow and title companies, inspectors, lenders, attorneys and, where legally required, regulators. We use service providers for email delivery and website hosting, who process data on our instruction only.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our site uses local storage to remember which listings you have saved. It does not use advertising cookies. If analytics are enabled on your installation, they are configured to collect aggregate data only.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for three years. Transaction records are kept for the period required by state licensing law, typically seven years after closing.

7. Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not required to retain it.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing at any time, using the link in every email.

To exercise any of these, email hello@everlinerealty.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use encrypted transmission, access controls and role-based permissions. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive financial documents by ordinary email without agreeing an alternative with your agent first.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting existing clients will be notified by email.
